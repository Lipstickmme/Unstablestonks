# UnstableStonks

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# Token pages are noindex in their own markup — thousands of thin, fast-changing
# pages for tokens that mostly won't exist next week. Disallowing them here too
# would stop crawlers ever READING that noindex, so they stay crawlable and
# simply ask not to be indexed. The same reasoning keeps the filtered views
# crawlable: they carry a canonical back to the home page.

# Nothing here is worth a crawl budget.
Disallow: /api/
Disallow: /_build/
Disallow: /*?q=
